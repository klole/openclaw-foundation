# Approval and authority

The owner configures the approval matrix during installation. Default categories: new agents/teams, money or accounts, public/customer output and irreversible actions need owner authorization. Existing explicit authorization may cover the exact next step. Internal reversible work stays with its responsible seat and COS.

An approval card names the exact object/action, before and after, scope, evidence, suggested cost with reasons, total exposure, expiry, rollback and decision owner. A changed payload needs a new decision. Silence is not approval. Never ask the owner to choose an arbitrary budget; propose one based on the job.

Owner pauses/stops are recorded with their actual scope and end, or no end when none was stated. Preserve the original words. Do not invent a 24-hour expiry for a permanent instruction. Record authorization and execution receipts separately.
